Model Trainer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Model Trainer is developed and published by Lee ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why it is used, and the choices you have. Contact: lwenyuan61@gmail.com

What the game sends and keeps

Your Steam account. When the game uses its built-in API, it asks Steam to confirm who you are and whether your account owns the game. Steam returns your Steam account ID and the ownership result. We use them to confirm ownership, to keep track of your built-in API credit, and to apply daily and per-minute limits.

What you type to her. Her replies are generated by a large language model. When you send a message, the game sends your message, the recent conversation and the game details needed for a reply to our relay server. The relay server forwards them to an AI provider and returns the reply. For most players the provider is DeepInfra; for players in mainland China it is usually DeepSeek. Messages from players outside mainland China are never sent to a provider in China. Our relay server does not store the content of your messages or of her replies.

Usage and credit records. For each Steam account, our relay server records how many replies were generated each day and how much built-in API credit has been used or bought.

Technical data. Your IP address is used briefly, in memory only, to block abuse; it is removed within a minute and is not stored. Your approximate country, based on your IP address or reported by Steam, is used only to choose the provider for each message and is not stored. Server logs record the time of each request, the last six digits of your Steam account ID, the response time, the amount of text processed and its cost. They do not contain the content of your messages.

On your computer. Your game saves, settings and conversation history are stored on your computer. If you choose to use your own API key, the key is stored only on your computer and is encrypted with Windows' built-in protection. Your messages then go directly from your computer to the provider you chose (DeepSeek, BigModel, Z.ai, OpenRouter or Nebius Token Factory) under that provider's terms. We do not receive that key or those messages.

Payments. Purchases are handled by Steam. We do not receive your payment details.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, we do not show advertising, and we do not use your messages to train AI models.

AI providers and where data is processed

Messages sent through the built-in API are processed by the AI provider under its own terms and privacy policy: DeepInfra (deepinfra.com) or DeepSeek (deepseek.com). Your information may therefore be processed outside your country, including in the United States and, for players in mainland China, in China. Our relay server runs on a rented cloud server.

Please do not share sensitive personal information, such as passwords, financial details or health information, in conversations.

How long we keep information

Usage and credit records are kept while the built-in API service is running, so that your remaining credit is not lost. Server logs are kept for no more than 30 days.

Your choices and rights

You can play without the built-in API by choosing your own API key, or no API, in the game's settings. You can ask for a copy of the records linked to your Steam account, or ask us to delete them, by emailing lwenyuan61@gmail.com with a link to your Steam profile. Deleting these records also removes any remaining built-in API credit. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, including the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
